THE HEART OF THE HOUSE IS HUNGRY

You died in this mansion, and your soul was caught in its Heart — a beating sigil deep in the cellar. Something else lives here too: a tall, patient thing that feeds on souls, walking four floors night after night. Eight embers of your passing are hidden through the house — two on each floor, never in the same place twice. Gather them, break the seal on the front door, and leave. If it takes you, the embers you carried scatter back into the dark, the seal re-forms, and the Heart drags you back down to the cellar to begin again.
